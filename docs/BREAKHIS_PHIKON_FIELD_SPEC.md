For direct prediction, average field probabilities within each magnification, then average available magnifications. For the two-step arm, first compose each field's joint distribution [1−r,rq_DC,rq_LC,rq_MC,rq_PC]. Average those joint probabilities within each magnification and across available magnifications. Derive the case malignant probability and conditional four-type distribution from the pooled joint probabilities. Route a case to malignant when its malignant probability is at least 0.5, then take the largest conditional malignant subtype probability. Logsumexp implements every average and conditional calculation for numerical stability. This joint-pooling choice was finalized by the project lead after the initial independent-head-pooling proposal.
