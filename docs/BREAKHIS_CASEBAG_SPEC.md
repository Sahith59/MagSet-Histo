## Scientific question and comparison scope

Test a small ImageNet CNN trained on **case-bag decisions**, rather than assigning a case label to every individual field. A sampled collection of fields feeds a shared picture reader, mean pooling and output heads. The direct arm predicts benign/DC/LC/MC/PC. The dual arm predicts benign/malignant, then DC/LC/MC/PC only for predicted malignant cases; benign stops. Two complete pipelines have the same encoder architecture and 6,425 output-head parameters each. Their losses and encoder gradients differ. This is a matched comparison of complete pipelines, not an isolated causal test of hierarchy.

This change is motivated by the earlier fitting–validation gap and weak image-level readouts, not proof that the reader or objective caused those errors. Mean pooling can dilute rare diagnostic fields, sampled bags may miss useful tissue, and only two lobular/three papillary cases fit the specialist. More epochs cannot create independent cases. The 94–95% goal is unachieved and cannot be forecast from this recipe. [Independent diagnoses](reviews/FIVE_CLASS_MODEL_DIAGNOSIS.md).

## Loss scaling: the exact contract

LetN=46, number of masksK=15, configured case batchB0=4. Final-five counts are11/25/2/5/3; binary counts11/35; malignant subtype counts25/2/5/3. Compute unreduced CE/BCE losses, multiply by these weights, sum over actual cases/masks, and **divide byB0×K=60**, including the final batch with two cases:

- Direct class weight `46/(5*n_final_class)`.
- Binary weight `46/(2*n_binary_group)`.
- Expert weight `46/(4*n_malignant_subtype)` for truly malignant cases; zero for benign.
- `Ldual = .5*(Lbinary + Lexpert)`. When a batch has no malignant cases, expert loss is zero connected to the router/shared graph and specialist parameters retain `grad=None`; with `zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`, AdamW skips unsupervised specialist weight decay/moment updates. Never take an empty mean, divide by malignant minibatch count/sum of weights, or add another46/35multiplier.

Expert weights use46, rather than35, because its numerator is averaged over **all**46cases, including zero benign contribution. Summed over a complete epoch, each of its four subtype classes has mass46/4 and each loss component has total mass46. Direct and binary classes similarly have equal per-head mass. The .5/.5combination makes total epoch objective mass46, matching direct. A fixed denominator60 gives each case the same base gradient scaling even in the short final batch; using its actual size2would double that batch's influence. Epoch logging separately sums numerators and divides by46×15, not by12batch means. These statements refer to fixed per-loss weighting, not identical optimizer trajectories or equivalent objectives.

No label smoothing, focal loss, auxiliary field loss, extra8-class weighting or threshold tuning. The specialist trains on true-malignant fitting labels, never on validation cases or the fitting router's predicted subset.

## Prospective review findings and remaining execution gates

The proposed expert scaling and fixed final-batch denominator are sound as stated. Paired stateless input sampling is necessary because different head architectures consume different RNG; one shared mutable RNG would silently break pairing. Selective BN affine adaptation with fixed buffers is supported by the pinned implementation, provided eval-mode and gradient settings are handled separately.

Remaining practical risks are the small number of independent cases, just600updates/model, a sampled-versus-all-field pooling distribution change, square aspect warp and headLR100times backboneLRbefore the shared decay. They are declared fixed choices, not opportunities to tune after outcomes. The source/environment/load/smoke checks remain open. This draft therefore remains **unregistered**.
