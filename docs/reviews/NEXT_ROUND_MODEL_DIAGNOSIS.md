## Training findings: a valid surrogate differs from the inference target

No invalidating training or probability-composition bug was found in the inspected paths. The reviewed kernel uses stabilized class/case/field density weights, removes the whole fitting query before centering/support, and composes joint field probabilities before pooling. P's new supervised centers are an ablation of valid geometry, not a correction of invalid priors. Native image parity and correct token geometry do not imply favorable representation quality; its result cannot isolate positional interpolation, field context or scale as a sole cause.

The actionable difference is in [midnight_lora.weighted_case_loss](../../magset/midnight_lora.py): it averages cross-entropy over eight sampled fields carrying the case label. Evaluation averages complete joint probabilities over all available fields and views. Therefore the registered LoRA surrogate encourages every sampled field to identify the case, even if fields differ in diagnostic content. That is mathematically valid, but differs from a case-supervised bag objective. Fixed normalization from the original checkpoint and augmentation/adaptation change the input distribution together; their causal effects were not separately identified. Eight of ten dual selections occur at head-only epoch 2. The study neither proves that adapters cannot help nor supplies evidence to repeat this package unchanged.

A label-free scalar check illustrates the objective distinction. Eight true-class field probabilities alternating .99 and .01 have mean-field CE 2.307610, while their pooled probability is .5 and bag CE .693147, a Jensen gap of 1.614463. This is synthetic arithmetic, not performance evidence. It demonstrates why the objectives differ; bag training can also hide bad individual fields and is not automatically better.

The earlier [EfficientNet case-bag model](../../magset/five_casebag.py) already used exact all-15 case losses, so bag supervision itself has been tested and failed in a different package. That model averaged two sampled fields per view, used an ImageNet reader with selected adaptation, and achieved only 46.50%/53.33% dual validation accuracy despite much higher fitting scores. The historical [HierarchicalFusion](../../magset/fusion.py) already implements gated field attention and mean/gated/attention view fusion on all fields. Its [training controller](../../magset/train_fusion.py) hardcodes eight labels and Phikon/ResNet inputs. Historical neural failures and scarce fitting cases are counterevidence against expecting a large gain from attention alone. A five-label frozen-Midnight comparison is untested, not a new algorithm.

## Recommend one experiment: frozen F fields, case loss, mean versus gated attention

Use only the accepted F three-crop 3,072-dimensional field features and original roles. Keep the encoder frozen with zero new encoder forwards. Apply the accepted fitting-only per-view mean/std normalizer; do not combine P centering, native features, extra crops or new augmentation into this experiment. Retain all fields rather than sampling two per view. No cross-magnification field correspondence is assumed.

The two readouts share `Linear(3072,128) → ReLU → Dropout(.25)` independently applied to fields. The matched mean control takes the arithmetic mean of projected fields per view. The candidate uses standard gated attention, with 32-dimensional tanh/sigmoid branches and a scalar score, softmax-normalized over valid fields in that view. Both take the equal mean of observed view embeddings, append the four availability flags, and use a linear 132→5 head for direct-five or 132→1 and 132→4 heads for dual. No view attention, distinct view classifiers, scale embeddings, auxiliary field CE, instance pseudo-labels or contrastive loss are added. At inference, compose the dual probabilities from the case router and expert; route malignant at R≥.5, including ties.

This has 394,009 mean-model parameters and 402,297 attention-model parameters; the attention increment is 8,288. These are small relative to the frozen encoder but substantial relative to 46 fitting cases. The proposal therefore needs the matched control, fixed regularization and complete negative-result reporting. Attention pooling and bag labels are established MIL methods, including [Ilse et al., ICML 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/ilse18a.html); attention-based pathology MIL also appears in [Lu et al., Nature Biomedical Engineering 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-020-00682-w). These sources motivate the method, not its expected score on this cohort. Attention weights alone do not establish a pathology explanation.

## Fixed training and selection proposal

- Fit each readout separately for direct/dual across all ten roles: **40 trajectories**, 160 candidate pipelines. Fit-only support remains 11/25/2/5/3; malignant-head loss uses only the 35 true malignant fitting cases.
- Use AdamW, learning rate 3e-4, weight decay .01, betas (.9,.999), epsilon 1e-8, gradient clipping at 1.0, 100 epochs and no warmup. Decrease learning rate by a fixed cosine from 3e-4 to 3e-5 over epochs 1–100; validation never changes it.
- Visit each of the 46 cases once per epoch in a common deterministic permutation; batch size four, no dropped final batch. Compute the field embeddings once per case/batch and reuse them across all 15 exact masks. Average mask losses equally. Use the fixed denominator four cases × 15 masks, including the final two-case batch.
- Direct case loss uses weight 46/(5×class count). Dual uses .5 times binary BCE weighted by 46/(2×group count), plus .5 times malignant CE weighted by 46/(4×malignant-type count). No malignant-head gradient or AdamW update arises from an all-benign batch. Shared projection/attention may learn from binary loss; the specialist head is supervised only by malignant cases.
- Save/evaluate epochs **10/25/50/100**, selecting the earliest maximum end-to-end mean15-subset macro F1 separately for each readout/arm/role. This is four opportunities per named readout. Selecting between mean and attention afterward creates eight family opportunities per arm; report both modes rather than hiding that choice.
- Use identical projection/head initial states across the two pool modes, component-keyed initialization, and a reset common training RNG before dropout. Keep data order and dropout streams paired across modes where shapes permit. FP32, TF32 disabled, deterministic kernels; no trainable image reader.

A proposed allocation is one A40, four CPU cores, 16 GB host RAM and 15 minutes per trajectory, at most four concurrent GPUs: ten allocated GPU hours maximum for production. This is a cap, not a measured runtime. Fitting-only technical checks must measure full-bag throughput/memory and exercise both poolers and both arms before admission. The cached input is small enough to avoid image decoding and a 4.5 GB model load; actual head-training time, not GPU count, determines its value.

## What the controls would resolve

Mean versus attention isolates learned field weighting under the same frozen features, projection, case objective, masks and selection opportunities. Both versus F/P compares complete classifier packages; it does not isolate the objective alone because nonlinear projection and feature pooling also change. Freeze F and P as the prospective scorecard references, report all four modes/arms and all ten roles, and retain the original descriptive screen and proper losses. Do not replace the masked endpoint with the all-four-view score.

Replay every saved candidate from accepted cached features and saved small weights, preferably with canonical CPU evaluation for hard-route consistency. Check all 15 masks, masked-value and field-permutation invariance, whole-case role isolation, fit-only normalization, zero benign expert gradients, finite extreme-logit behavior, save/reload, exact candidate selection and public/private artifact separation. Fitting scores here are in-sample neural diagnostics; do not directly compare them to KDE leave-case-out fitting diagnostics as if their conditions matched.

Patch-token MIL is deferred because existing caches retain CLS+patch means, not patch distributions; it would add extraction/storage and change the representation simultaneously. RBF SVM/nonlinear kernel readouts remain plausible, but KDE is already nonlinear and those alternatives do not directly test field relevance or the field/case objective difference. Separate magnification heads multiply scarce-class boundaries while same-view KDE already supplies view-conditional densities. Further encoder adaptation is more expensive and has weaker current evidence than testing a readout on the accepted strongest frozen features.

The main risk remains statistical: two lobular and three papillary fitting cases per role cannot constrain a flexible model reliably, and stain/case artifacts may receive high attention. This experiment can reject or support one controlled readout hypothesis on reused development data. It cannot promise 95%, new patients, clinical readiness or algorithmic novelty.
