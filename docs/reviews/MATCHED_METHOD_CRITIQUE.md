# Matched study: methodological critique and bounded next step

November 17, 2026. **The strongest local evidence favors testing a different frozen malignant representation before fitting another large network.** It does not establish a resolution defect, incorrect labels, a new learning mechanism or a model capable of >90% final-five and >97% binary accuracy. Those remain measured-performance objectives. This review proposes one development experiment; it executes no fitting, image inference or scheduler work.

## What the evidence supports

The completed matched comparison is technically accepted, but its primary point estimates remain modest: P 67.50%/.375675, CNN 56.67%/.348180 and Swin 59.17%/.304947 final-five accuracy/macro F1. All pairwise intervals include zero; that does not demonstrate equivalence. P's 94.58% binary result is a separate first-stage endpoint. Its 13 routing and 65 subsequent typing errors are useful descriptive locations, not proof of a biological cause. [Accepted matched results](../MATCHED_THREE_WAY_RESULTS.md).

The separate fitting/validation audit found no demonstrated label-map, input-scale, loss-mask or pooling defect. Selected CNN online fitting binary/malignant CE is .02169/.09276 versus independently reconstructed validation CE 1.53746/4.06287; Swin is .00548/.08264 versus 1.75088/3.94068. Online augmented fitting loss and fixed-checkpoint validation loss are different measurements, and no fitting accuracy is inferred. Nevertheless, successful fitting with weak, often confident validation predictions argues against simply extending the same optimization. [Training/validation diagnosis](MATCHED_TRAINING_DIAGNOSIS.md).

Evidence predating that viewed test offers a stronger rationale for a specialist experiment. Frozen UNI2-h improved older arrangement-812 malignant typing to 72.00%, versus C's 56.67%, but its routing was worse; the improvement did not consistently carry to arrangement 813. UNI2-h was not uniformly better than P. Its supervised linear readout subsequently lost typing/F1, and bounded diagonal metric learning gave only small gains. These findings support a limited representation-complementarity hypothesis, not a new winner. [Reader comparison](../BREAKHIS_APPROVED_ENCODERS_RESULTS.md), [linear negative result](../BREAKHIS_UNI_LINEAR_RESULTS.md), [metric results](../BREAKHIS_SPECIALIST_METRIC_RESULTS.md). The original UNI paper and current UNI2-h author documentation support evaluating frozen pathology features with simple readouts; neither supplies a BreakHis five-category performance guarantee. [UNI](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-02857-3), [UNI2-h model documentation](https://huggingface.co/MahmoodLab/UNI2-h).

The alternative explanations have weaker or contrary local evidence:

- **Resolution/coverage:** three-crop coverage gave small mixed gains; the registered native 700×448 intervention fell to 60%/61%. That test changed context, resolution and positional interpolation together. It rejects that particular larger-input recipe, not all native-tile approaches. A cropping cause remains unproven. [Coverage](../BREAKHIS_COVERAGE_RESULTS.md), [native-input result](../BREAKHIS_NATIVE_RESULTS.md).
- **More learned capacity or attention:** case-MIL selected fitting accuracy was about 99%, yet validation remained weak. LoRA scarcely improved its already weak initialization, often selecting the head-only checkpoint. This is evidence of limited transfer for those recipes, not insufficient ability to fit. Attention MIL is established prior art, and its success elsewhere does not reverse these local negatives. [MIL](../BREAKHIS_CASE_MIL_RESULTS.md), [LoRA](../BREAKHIS_LORA_RESULTS.md), [Ilse et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/ilse18a.html).
- **Label/tissue-content problems:** not adjudicated. A specimen label does not establish that every field contains diagnostic subtype morphology. The dataset paper describes fields that can contain transitional tissue. Review training/validation fields before asserting staining, label or morphology causes; do not silently relabel difficult test cases. [Dataset paper](https://www.inf.ufpr.br/lesoliveira/download/TBME-00608-2015-R2-preprint.pdf).

## Evaluation limits that another model cannot remove

There are 82 provider specimens, 81 bare identifiers and 70 conservative stem groups, with unresolved clinical identity. Preserve separate specimen labels within mixed-label groups and put every related field/crop/view in one role. The registered roles remain 49 fitting/17 validation/16 test specimens; fitting has only two lobular groups, while validation's two lobular specimens occupy one group. Test has one lobular and one papillary specimen. Hundreds of fields or augmentations do not create additional independent subtype diversity.

All 15 subsets remain required. The 240 test specimen/subset records are dependent; choosing an easier magnification or reporting only P's perfect all-four-view binary routing would change the question. Fixed-label bootstrap intervals are fragile here: 1,533/2,000 resamples omit a truth class. Pretraining, crop coverage, compute and magnification use also differ between pipelines, so the matched outcome does not isolate backbone architecture.

The current 16-case test has now been viewed. Preserve its original metrics and roles. A new method must not be chosen or tuned against those outcomes; rerunning it later cannot create fresh confirmation. New folds over previously explored cases also remain development evidence. Proper nested/grouped assessment must contain all model selection within its training folds, and cannot retrospectively erase this project's prior exposure. [Varma and Simon](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1397873/), [Dwork et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa9375).

MagViT's reported patient figure is for its binary task, not a five-category target to borrow. The same distinction applies to the supplied CNN's image-random eight-type result. [MagViT primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.16959v1).

## Recommended single family: fixed-router specialist composition

This is an engineering hypothesis from the **pre-matched** evidence, not novelty or a response optimized to the viewed test errors.

1. **Keep the current 49 fitting and 17 validation specimens, all four views and all 15 subsets.** Use no current-test outputs during development. Freeze the family, candidate order, primary comparison, progression rule and complete hashes before evaluating its validation candidates.
2. **Fix the P binary router and give both specialist arms the same budget.** Keep binary temperature 0.1 and its fitting-only head-specific centers/support. Recompute from the current 49-case fitting role or verify exact replay; never reuse centers fitted under older 46-case roles. Keep its field binary probabilities authoritative. The P malignant control and UNI2-h malignant candidate each receive four malignant-temperature choices. The P control at malignant temperature 0.1 must reproduce the original parent pipeline. Do not add a router, threshold, ensemble-weight or binary-temperature search.
3. **Use the accepted frozen UNI2-h raw 1,536-dimensional field cache.** Align every field by canonical relative path, image SHA, provider specimen, group and magnification. Assert complete one-to-one membership; never zip independently ordered cache arrays. Disclose UNI2-h's center crop versus P's three-crop mean and the two-reader compute/pretraining differences.
4. **Preserve the accepted UNI2-h KDE geometry.** In each view, compute every fitting case's raw-field mean in float64, then take the equal mean of the 49 case means as the center. Center each raw field and divide by `max(L2 norm, 1e-12)`. Use only true-malignant fitting cases as malignant support. Each subtype density gives equal mass to its support cases and equal field mass within each case/view. No learned metric, standardizer, PCA, query-batch statistic or new centering mode enters this family.
5. **Evaluate exactly four specialist temperatures per arm:** 0.01/0.03/0.1/0.3, shared across magnifications. Compose each field's fixed P binary probabilities with that arm's conditional four-type probabilities; then use the unchanged joint-before-field/view pooling and 0.5 cascade. Select each arm's first maximum validation mean-15 final-five macro F1, giving eight visible candidate pipelines altogether. Preserve every candidate, including failures. The prospective development progression screen retains the earlier +.02 F1/nondecreasing-accuracy rule against the separately selected P control; show every class recall and do not substitute a target-accuracy preference after seeing results.
6. **Require type-only invariance and independent replay.** Check exact control decisions, finite normalized densities, field/path alignment, fitting-only centers/support, benign expert masking, crop/cache provenance, field-order and inactive-view invariance, probability/decision replay and save/reload. Binary field/case marginals and hard routes must match P under a registered numerical tolerance; an unexplained route change invalidates the isolated specialist claim. Save source/configuration/cache/split/center/support/candidate/selection/probability hashes and independently replay all validation metrics before any follow-up query.

By construction this family leaves P's binary performance unchanged. It cannot demonstrate progress toward >97% binary accuracy. A separate **training-only leave-whole-group-out router diagnostic** can justify a later intervention: exclude the queried stem from every fitted center, support, model and calibration statistic, not merely its last nearest neighbor. Such a diagnostic is not a new final result.

After selection, freeze both selected candidates before any follow-up score. A repeat on the already viewed 16-case role must be labeled a retrospective reused-development comparison, with all 15 subsets and unchanged old results. A genuinely independent confirmation of >90% final-five/>97% binary performance requires new appropriately grouped, adequately represented cases; there is no globally fresh BreakHis cohort left in this project. If no such cohort is available, report the bounded validation/development result honestly rather than manufacture confirmation through resplitting or zoom selection. The concrete [registered specialist-reader plan](../BREAKHIS_SPECIALIST_READER_PLAN.md) and [configuration](../../configs/breakhis-specialist-reader-v1.json) fix this family before execution.
