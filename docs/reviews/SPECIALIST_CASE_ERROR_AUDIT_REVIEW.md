## Three different summaries

- Original headline accuracy remains the original **600 dependent records per arrangement**, and original macro F1 remains the mean of subset/trial F1 values. Pooling both arrangements into 1,200 descriptive events does not replace either headline or create independent replication.
- `event_weighted_error_rate` divides total errors by total observed events. Provider keys occurring in more reused roles contribute more events.
- `equal_provider_key_mean_error_rate` averages each distinct key's observed errors/exposures equally within the reported scope. This changes descriptive weighting; it does not produce a new majority-vote case prediction, a fresh patient result, a class-balanced metric or the original macro F1. Empty scopes report no rate.

The paired correction/regression partition and confusion matrices describe dependent events. Error-concentration rankings explicitly use raw error-event burden and retain the matching exposure counts. A highly exposed key can rank higher without having a higher error rate. Such rankings prioritize review of observed errors; they do not establish biological difficulty or independent clinical failure risk.

The subtype pattern explains why the linear router's gain does not resolve the task.
