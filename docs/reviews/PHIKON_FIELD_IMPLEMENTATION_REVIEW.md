The root's final choice composes each field's five-class distribution `[1-r, r*q]` before pooling. Fields receive equal weight within their own case/view; available views receive equal weight. The pooled case router probability is the sum of the four pooled malignant probabilities. Its conditional malignant distribution divides those probabilities by their malignant total in stable log space. Algebraically the specialist readout is `E[r*q] / E[r]`, rather than `E[q]`. Its predictive benefit is unmeasured.

The hard decision routes malignant at pooled `R >= 0.5`, then chooses the largest conditional malignant component. Benign stops. The argmax of the composed five-vector can disagree with that decision; score selection and classification metrics use the saved explicit hard decision. Stable log-sum-exp means keep finite malignant conditionals when ordinary malignant probabilities underflow. No physical correspondence between similarly numbered fields across zooms is assumed.
