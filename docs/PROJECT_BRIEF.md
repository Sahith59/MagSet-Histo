# MAGSET-Histo: current BreakHis project brief

For the current BreakHis dual-step capstone, the original case-first UNI2-h model scored 82.92% five-category and 96.25% binary on the 16-case internal benchmark. The newer subtype-initialization experiment improved one validation comparison but lost five-category accuracy at all three additional training seeds. [Professor brief and matched comparison](evidence/PROFESSOR_BRIEF.md).

Keep C, F and both P modes in the scorecard. Head-specific P retains the highest descriptive F1 **among two-step systems**: 813 accuracy remains below F's 76.83%, and both P modes fail the registered +.02 F1/nondecreasing-accuracy screen. GenBio's stronger one-stage 813 score is not the requested dual result. The original 93.73%/94.99% notebook outputs used a different eight-type, 40× image-random reused-validation protocol and do not measure this five-category case task.

N's native-input recipe did not help. P tests supervised centering, with four unique direct and eight dual settings across its mode family; the original center remains valid. Whole-case MIL compares mean and gated field attention under the same objective. Its high fitting scores, active gradients and weak validation support a generalization-gap diagnosis; sparse rare cases remain a limitation, not a proven unique cause. With GenBio's current case-conditional subtype predictions fixed, perfect routing would reach only 80.00%/83.83% final accuracy. Binary scores above 95% do not solve the final task.

These are standard methods, without an established novelty claim. Any further malignant-specialist objective or approved-encoder study needs separate registration and matched controls; gated-model access remains unconfirmed. No automatic router/expert hybrid, N+P combination, resplit or tuning sweep follows. Availability-conditioned evidence matching and TCGA expansion remain deferred, and no 95% outcome is promised.
